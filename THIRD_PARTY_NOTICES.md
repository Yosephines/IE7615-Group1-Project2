# Sources and licenses

COCO annotations belong to the COCO Consortium and are provided under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the [COCO downloads](https://cocodataset.org/#download) and [terms](https://cocodataset.org/#termsofuse). Original captions, selected annotation IDs and factual corrections were retained in the manifest and review records.

Images belong to their original copyright holders. This subset retained COCO license IDs 4 and 5: Attribution and Attribution-ShareAlike. Each manifest row records the Flickr source and image-specific license. RGB conversion, resizing and padding were the image transformations. Retain the source and license information with shared reference images.
